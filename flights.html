GETTING TO ROAD TOWN

Road Town is the capital of the British Virgin Islands, on Tortola — the largest island in the chain. The Moorings base sits just north of the town center at Wickhams Cay II, and that's where our boat will be waiting.

Each couple books their own airfare. Read both airport explainers first — service and what it takes to reach the boat differ drastically.

EIS
Terrance B. Lettsome · Beef Island, Tortola

A small island airport with a short runway — no widebodies, so the schedule is thin. One US nonstop: American out of Miami. Everything else connects through San Juan on a turboprop or a 9-seater.

GOOD
You land on Tortola. 25-minute taxi to the marina, aboard the same afternoon. No boat, no second island, no extra $500.
COST
Fares run roughly $100–260 more per person, and from Jackson or Chattanooga it's usually two layovers. Small planes mean tighter bag limits.
STT
Cyril E. King · St. Thomas, USVI

The region's big airport. Full-size jets and nonstops from Miami, Atlanta, Charlotte and Houston on American, Delta and United — more departures, more seats, cheaper fares.

GOOD
Cheapest and most frequent way into the region, often a single layover from all three home airports.
CATCH
It's a different island and a different country. You still have to cross to Road Town: public ferry, 45 min, about $60 a couple, last one 3:30p. Land later and it's a private water taxi, ≈$500 a couple. Either way you clear BVI customs on arrival.
